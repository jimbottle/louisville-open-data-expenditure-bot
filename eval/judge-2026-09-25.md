# Judged pass over the warmed starters — 2026-09-25

The 11 cached starter answers in production were graded as a judge would
(A correct and useful … D wrong), prompts were adjusted, and all 11 were
re-asked live against the new prompts three times (one sample per run, so
single-run changes below the noise are not claimed).

| # | Starter | Before | After | What moved it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Annual spend trend | B | A | Summarize instead of transcribing 19 rows; name turning points |
| 2 | Top agencies | B | A | Top 5, rounded; vendor-payments note |
| 3 | LMPD year-over-year | C | B | Table note: vendor payments, payroll excluded (LMPD pay ≈ $145M CY2025 vs $43M expenditures) |
| 4 | Mayor & chief pay | A | A | Calendar-year fact (a run said "fiscal year") + pay note |
| 5 | Highest-paid positions | B | A | No duplicated fields / avg=max boilerplate |
| 6 | Registered agents | A | A | — |
| 7 | Capital projects | C | A- | Legislation only when it explains a figure; no document amounts |
| 8 | Grant funding | D | B+ | Data showed it is spending from grant-named funds, not receipts: fact + table note, and the starter no longer asserts "received" (the model followed the question's premise over the fact in 3/3 runs) |
| 9 | Vendors across agencies | A- | A- | — |
| 10 | Contract splitting | C | B | Deterministic caveat (leads, not evidence; routine same-size payments); refine: describe records, never motives. The prompt-only neutrality rule held on one run and failed on the next |
| 11 | Tech & cybersecurity | B | B | Variance is in the generated SQL (different "technology" definitions per run), not the prose |

Lesson: prompts moved tone and presentation reliably, but not load-bearing
caveats — those are now deterministic (city.yaml `table_notes`, the
irregularity notice). Accuracy eval after the change: 23/23
(`results/20260925-113831-judge-pass-prompts.md`), unchanged from baseline.
